Small Document: Equipment Standards Guide

GARMIN DEVICE SPECIFICATIONS
Garmin GPS devices require specific performance standards for optimal operation. These devices must meet accuracy requirements of within 3 meters under open sky conditions.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
The equipment standards include:
- Signal acquisition time under 30 seconds
- Battery life minimum 8 hours continuous operation
- Operating temperature range -20°C to 60°C
- Water resistance rating IPX7 or higher

CALIBRATION PROCEDURES
Regular calibration ensures device accuracy:
- Monthly GPS accuracy verification
- Compass calibration before each mission
- Altitude sensor adjustment for local conditions
- Software updates as recommended by manufacturer

MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
Equipment maintenance requirements:
- Weekly visual inspection for damage
- Monthly battery and charging system check
- Quarterly firmware updates
- Annual professional service and certification

These standards ensure reliable operation in all operational environments and maintain accuracy for critical navigation tasks.
